# Surface Brief — AegisPay Dispute Operations Workbench

Scope: `client/src/App.jsx` and all primary workspace views (`DisputeGrid.jsx`, `AIAnalyzer.jsx`, `BankIntegration.jsx`, `PolicyStudio.jsx`, `OracleSyndicate.jsx`, `EvidenceModal.jsx`).
Visitor Mode: `operate`

## Audience & Job
Primary user is a PayPal merchant risk & dispute operations analyst working a live queue of friendly fraud (INR), unauthorized card claims, and SNAD inquiries under strict bank response deadlines. Secondary audience is PayPal AI Hackathon judges evaluating the multi-agent architecture, AG Grid integration, and real-time evidence compilation.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every PayPal dispute is a forensic case file whose physical carrier proof, network telemetry, and legal rebuttal are tagged, sealed, and signed before dispatch—refusing the generic dark-mode neon-purple SaaS template in favor of an authoritative institutional workbench.

OWN-WORLD: Deep institutional navy left command rail (`#0F1D35`), warm archival stone canvas (`#F4F3EF`) with crisp paper surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) and structural 1px hairlines (`#D8D6CE`), paired with warm manila exhibit tags (`#FBF6E9` ground, `#DFC99B` border, `#8A5A00` stamp) reserved strictly for verified evidentiary artifacts, PayPal institutional blue (`#005EA6`) for primary actions, crimson (`#B3261E`) for statutory deadlines, and forest green (`#166534`) for Seller Protection seals. Typography pairs `Geist` (structural UI & display) with `Geist Mono` (tabular case IDs, GPS coordinates, dollar figures, and REST payloads).

STORY: The analyst sees the entire dispute exposure and deadline pressure at a glance, selects or filters any case in the AG Grid queue, inspects the 5-agent forensic exhibit chain (Sentinel, Sherlock, Oracle, Lex, Pax) in a split-pane or live workbench, and dispatches a Visa CE 3.0 / PayPal Seller Protection rebuttal packet in one decisive action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Fixed 248px institutional navy left navigation rail with live sandbox status and workspace switcher; top operational ledger bar showing real-time disputed exposure, Seller Protection eligibility, and a "Simulate Dispute Attack" trigger; main workspace featuring the AG Grid Dispute Queue with proportional deadline bars and an integrated right-hand Case Exhibit & Rebuttal Inspector drawer when a row is active.

FORM: Candidate 4 — Chain of Custody & Forensic Exhibit Workbench (`seed:d8df6602ab14246b`).
